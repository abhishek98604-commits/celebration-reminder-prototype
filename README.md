# Celebration reminder · interactive prototype

A celebration reminder app I designed for a client in 2017–18, rebuilt as a clickable, mobile-first prototype for my portfolio.

- **Live prototype:** https://abhishek98604-commits.github.io/celebration-reminder-prototype/
- **Phone-frame version (for embedding):** https://abhishek98604-commits.github.io/celebration-reminder-prototype/embed.html

## What you can do

- Browse the calendar by month and tap a date to see who is celebrating
- Tap a person to text a ready-made wish, "call" them, or send a gift card
- See the gift cards you received and redeem them
- Check reminders and add a new birthday, anniversary or wedding

## Notes

- Colours, layout and the original faces come from the 2017–18 design file. Avenir is swapped for the free Nunito Sans.
- People, messages, phone numbers and gift cards are sample content. No calls, messages or payments happen.
- One HTML file, no build step. State lives in memory and resets on reload.
